02Service description

NetGateManager is an Android utility application designed to monitor, analyze, and manage compatible cellular routers (including ZTE and Huawei 4G and 5G models). Key functionalities include:

  • Real-time cellular metrics: Reading and displaying signal indicators (such as RSRP, RSRQ, SINR, RSSI), connected cell identity, PCI, frequency bands, and carrier aggregation status.
  • Router configuration: Cell locking (PCI/EARFCN locking), frequency band locking, network mode selection (Auto/5G/4G), APN configuration, Wi-Fi radio settings, and administrative reboots.
  • Local utilities: Reading and sending SMS messages stored on the router SIM card, connected Wi-Fi client management, Wi-Fi spectrum analysis, and internet speed tests.

All router communications take place directly between your Android device and the local gateway address of your router (for example, 192.168.0.1 or 192.168.8.1) over your local Wi-Fi or LAN connection.

04Router configuration & technical risks

NetGateManager provides advanced tools that interact directly with your router's hardware and cellular modem firmware:

Cell & Band Locking Caution: Locking your router to specific cellular frequencies (bands) or specific tower identifiers (PCI / Cell Lock) forces the modem to reject all other towers. If the selected cell or band experiences an outage, changes parameters, or provides insufficient signal, your router may lose cellular connection completely until the lock is released or the router is factory reset.

By using configuration and write features, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • Sole responsibility: You are solely responsible for any settings or configuration changes applied to your router. We are not liable for any lost internet connectivity, telecommunications provider penalties, or disruptions caused by your settings.
  • Restoring connectivity: If a configuration change leaves your router unable to connect to cellular service, you may need to reconnect locally via Wi-Fi or Ethernet cable to remove the lock, or perform a manual physical factory reset (via the router's hardware reset pinhole).
  • Firmware variations: Router manufacturers and cellular carriers regularly release firmware updates. A carrier firmware update may modify, restrict, or remove administrative endpoints without notice. While we continuously verify and update compatibility profiles, we cannot guarantee that every feature will work across all firmware builds or carrier-customized variants.
